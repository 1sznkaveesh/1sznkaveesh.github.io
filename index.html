Research

Taking published legged-robot methods apart to see what's really doing the work.

Can a robot's safety switch learn on the job?

Building on Agile But Safe (He et al., RSS 2024) · Isaac Gym, Unitree Go1

ABS keeps a fast-running legged robot safe with a small learned "safety value" that decides when to hand control to a cautious recovery policy. It breaks when goals are farther away than anything it saw in training, so I tried retraining it online. That fixed the far-goal case (false-safe 0.97% → 0.01%) but made everyday safety about 7× worse. The same trade-off showed up again on a second, independently trained policy, and none of the three fixes I tried got around it.

What is DreamWaQ's "imagination" actually imagining?

Reproducing DreamWaQ (Nahrendra et al., ICRA 2023) · Isaac Gym, Unitree Go2

DreamWaQ gives a walking robot a learned latent that is meant to capture the terrain under its feet. After reproducing it, I went looking for that terrain information and couldn't find it. The latent had collapsed to its prior (0 of 16 dimensions active), so the robot was effectively walking blind. I also tested a fix for its velocity-estimation gap. It failed the criteria I'd set in advance, so I rejected it.
